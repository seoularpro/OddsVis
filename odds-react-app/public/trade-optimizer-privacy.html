OddsVis Trade Optimizer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

OddsVis Trade Optimizer is a Chrome extension that analyzes the fantasy football league you have open and suggests trades. This page explains what data it touches and where it goes.

What the extension reads

  • League data from the fantasy site you are viewing. On ESPN and Sleeper league pages the extension reads league settings, rosters, and the free-agent list through the site's own API, using the browser session you are already signed in with. This happens inside your browser only.
  • Projections and trade values. Public data files are downloaded from the OddsVis GitHub repository. No information about you is included in those requests.

What leaves your browser

  • Nothing about your league or rosters is sent to OddsVis or any other server. All analysis runs locally in the extension.
  • License key only. If you purchase the Pro tier and enter a license key, the key and a short device label (for example "Chrome macOS") are sent to Lemon Squeezy, the payment provider, to confirm the key is valid. Lemon Squeezy's handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy.
  • Purchases happen on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page in a normal browser tab. The extension never sees payment details.

What is stored

  • Your settings (chosen team per league, optimizer tolerances, optional custom dataset URLs) and your license status are stored with Chrome's extension storage and may sync across your Chrome profile.
  • A trimmed copy of Sleeper's public player list may be cached locally for up to 24 hours to avoid re-downloading it.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are collected.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: shows the analysis panel.
  • storage: saves settings and license status.
  • activeTab and scripting: talk to the league page you are viewing, and inject the reader if the page was opened before the extension was installed.
  • Host access to fantasy.espn.com, sleeper.com, api.sleeper.app, raw.githubusercontent.com, and api.lemonsqueezy.com: read league data, download the datasets, and validate license keys. Yahoo is recognized but not read.

Your choices

Removing the extension deletes its stored settings and license status from Chrome. To deactivate a license on a device, use "Remove key" in the panel.
